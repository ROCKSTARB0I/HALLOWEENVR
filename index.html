Haddonfield, Illinois · October 31, 1978

You are Michael Myers.
Fifteen years ago, on this night, you came home.
You've come home again.

Building Haddonfield…

Quest. Left stick walks. You never run, but when nobody is watching you, you close the distance faster. Right stick turns. Your right hand holds the knife: stab hard and it goes in. Left grip on a throat strangles and lifts. A opens doors and gets into cars. Right grip lets go of a knife stuck in a wall; grip the handle to take it back. B pauses.

Desktop. WASD walk, mouse look, click to stab, hold right mouse to grab a throat, E for doors and cars, Q to tilt your head, N for free camera, Esc to pause.

The night. Annie is at the Wallace house across from the Doyles. Lynda and Bob are coming over. Laurie is babysitting at the Doyle house. Dr. Loomis is watching your old house, and he is armed. If they see you, they run, they lock doors, they call Sheriff Brackett. Stay in the dark. Be patient. Dawn ends it.
